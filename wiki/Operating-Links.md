# Operating links

Where meteor scatter stations meet, arrange skeds and see who is on the air. YA-MS tells you when and where to point; these sites help you find someone to work.

| Site | What it is for | Area |
|---|---|---|
| [ON4KST Chat](http://www.on4kst.com/chat/start.php) | Chat to arrange skeds: 144/432 MHz in Region 1, 50 MHz in Region 1 and Region 2 | Europe, Americas |
| [Ping Jockey](https://www.pingjockey.net/cgi-bin/pingtalk) | Meteor scatter chat and skeds, busiest during the showers | North America |
| [N6SJV](https://www.n6sjv.org/) | West Coast VHF group: some meteor scatter every day | USA, West Coast |
| [PSKReporter](https://pskreporter.info/pskmap.html) | Map of decodes: filter on MSK144 to see who is active; filter on your call as sender to see who hears you, even stations that are only listening | Worldwide |
| [DXMaps](https://www.dxmaps.com/spots/mapg.php) | Map of DX cluster spots, SSB and CW included: filter on the band | Worldwide |

Links taken from the meteor scatter slides by N9JIM (50 MHz and Up, August 2021). YA-MS already reads PSKReporter and the DX clusters in the Data collection tab.
