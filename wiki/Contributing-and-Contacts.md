## Reporting a problem

Open an **issue on GitHub** or write an e-mail with:

- operating system and YA-MS version (in the window title);
- the two locators, band and UTC time;
- what you expected and what you saw (a screenshot helps);
- for radar problems, the message shown in the [Radar flux](Radar-Flux) tab.

## What is especially useful

- **On-air reports.** Hours and paths where the efficiency or the forecast were right – or wrong. The model is experimental and needs comparison with real QSOs and with MCG_MS / MS-Soft.
- **RMOB comparisons.** Values of the station factor K and of the exponent k you found with the [RMOB comparison](Experimental-Forecasts#rmob-comparison), with your band and station.
- **Data with a source.** Better radiant drifts, activity profiles, new IMO peak dates (`data/massimi_imo.csv`), antenna data: include the source and it will be cited.
- **Changes of the radar charts.** If the Collm chart format changes, a copy of the image (`data/radar/`) helps to recalibrate the reading.

## Translations

The interface translations are plain text files in **`data/lingue/<code>.txt`** (UTF-8) and **anyone can correct them**:

```
<Italian text><TAB><translation>
```

- One entry per line; lines starting with `#` are comments.
- In both columns the two characters `\n` mean a line break.
- Keep the placeholders `{0}`, `{1}`, … with their format (e.g. `{0:F1}`, `{3:+0;-0}`), in the order your language needs.
- Missing or empty entries are shown in Italian. The list of all keys is `data/lingue/chiavi.txt` (generated by `tools/estrai_chiavi.py`, do not edit it).

Edit the file in the `data/lingue` folder of your installation and restart YA-MS (or switch language) to see the result. Because these files are **replaced by newer versions at each update**, please also **send your corrections** – by e-mail or as a pull request on GitHub (`data/lingue/`) – so they go into the next release.
