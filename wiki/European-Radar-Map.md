# Map of European radars

*Tab: Technical documents › Map of European radars.*

[![Map of European radars](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ic8tem/YA-MS/main/docs/screenshots/en-3_2.png)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ic8tem/YA-MS/main/docs/screenshots/en-3_2.png)

Where the sources of the live data are, relative to **your station** (the star, from *My locator*):

| Source | What it is | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| **Collm** (Germany) | SKiYMET meteor radar, 36.2 MHz | [Radar flux](Radar-Flux) |
| **Sodankylä** (Finland) | meteor radar, 36.9 MHz | [Radar flux](Radar-Flux) |
| **GRAVES** (France) | transmitter, 143.050 MHz | [GRAVES radar](GRAVES-Radar) |
| **CMOR** (Ontario, Canada) | meteor radar 17–38 MHz, orbits | [Live showers](Live-Showers) |

The **dashed circles** are the sky seen by each radar (about 300 km around the station, trails at 90 km): no radar looks at the sky above your path; the data say how active the shower is. **Dotted lines** give the distance from your station. Yellow triangles mark some Italian RMOB observers listening to GRAVES. The **Global Meteor Network** is not a point on the map but a worldwide camera network, described in its own box.
