# Live showers

*Tab: Live showers.*

[![Live showers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ic8tem/YA-MS/main/docs/screenshots/en-4.png)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ic8tem/YA-MS/main/docs/screenshots/en-4.png)

Two external sources, shown as images (they do not publish numbers to read):

- **Global Meteor Network (GMN)** – [globalmeteornetwork.org/flux](https://globalmeteornetwork.org/flux/): measured flux and **ZHR** of the active showers from a worldwide network of (mostly amateur) cameras, updated once a day; the red line marks the last update. The program reads the page, finds the active showers and shows the graph of the current year for each, plus the yearly ZHR overview. Data **CC BY 4.0**; cite Vida et al. 2021, MNRAS 506(4), and Vida et al. 2022, MNRAS 515(2).
- **CMOR**, Canadian Meteor Orbit Radar (University of Western Ontario, NASA Meteoroid Environment Office) – [fireballs.ndc.nasa.gov/cmor-radiants](https://fireballs.ndc.nasa.gov/cmor-radiants/): sky map of the radiants detected by the radar in near real time, including the **daytime showers**, and the same map coloured by speed. CMOR is at 43° N, about the latitude of southern Europe, so its sky is also ours.

Each file is downloaded again only if it has changed, at most every 30 minutes or with **Download now**.
